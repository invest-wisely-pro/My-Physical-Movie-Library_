# My Physical Movie Library

A free, private web app to catalogue your **physical film collection** (4K, Blu-ray, DVD) — in **English** and **Italian** (it follows your device language; switch it any time in ⚙️ Tools).

**Features**
- Shelf of clickable posters, filters, search, barcode scanner, loans, wishlist, box sets
- Behind the scenes (crew, awards, budget and box office, saga, age rating), critical reception from Wikipedia
- Director and actor profiles with filmography, career stats and frequent collaborators
- Related and recommended films, *What's your mood tonight?*, *Demo night*, collection statistics and trivia
- Works offline once installed; installable on Android and iPhone; Kodi/Plex/Jellyfin .nfo export

**Privacy** — your collection stays **on your device**. This site only serves the empty app: nothing you add is uploaded anywhere. Data comes from free, open sources (Wikidata, Wikipedia); no accounts, no API keys, no ads.

**Install** — open the page on your phone: Android (Chrome) → menu ⋮ → *Install app*; iPhone (Safari) → Share → *Add to Home Screen*. Then use *📤 Restore a backup* inside the installed app if you already have one.
